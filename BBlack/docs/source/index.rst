Welcome to BBLack's documentation!
==================================


BBlack is a bayesian analysis tool, aiming to compare astrophysical models for binary black hole populations with gravitational wave data.

BBlack is a program developed by Carole Périgois (carolperigois@outlook.com), with the support of from the European Research Council for the ERC Consolidator grant DEMOBLACK, under contract no. 770017._



.. _`DEMOBLACK`:
   https://demoblack.com/
